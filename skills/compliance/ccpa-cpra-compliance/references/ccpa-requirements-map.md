# CCPA requirements map

A compact map from review topics to the CCPA sections (Civ. Code §1798.100 et seq.) and CalPrivacy regulations (Cal. Code Regs. tit. 11, §7000 et seq.) behind them. It summarizes; it does not replace the text. Quote or paraphrase the section itself in a finding, and open the source when the exact wording matters. Amounts and dates below were checked on 2026-10-28.

## Official sources

- [CalPrivacy regulations page](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/): current regulation text, the CCPA statute as amended, Delete Act text, and open rulemakings. The California Privacy Protection Agency now calls itself CalPrivacy.
- [CCPA statute, California Legislative Information](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?division=3.&part=4.&lawCode=CIV&title=1.81.5): the authoritative statute text. The site may refuse scripted requests; open it in a browser.
- [CCPA regulations effective 2026-01-01 (PDF)](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/pdf/ccpa_statute_eff_20260101.pdf): the consolidated §7000-§7304 text, including cybersecurity audits, risk assessments and ADMT.
- [California Attorney General: CCPA](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa): the AG enforces the CCPA alongside CalPrivacy.
- [Global Privacy Control specification](https://w3c.github.io/gpc/): the `Sec-GPC: 1` header and `navigator.globalPrivacyControl` property, the opt-out preference signal businesses meet in practice.
- CalPrivacy enforcement advisories: [2024-01, data minimization in consumer requests](https://cppa.ca.gov/pdf/enfadvisory202401.pdf) and [2024-02, dark patterns](https://cppa.ca.gov/pdf/enfadvisory202402.pdf).

## Key definitions (§1798.140)

| Term                        | Subdivision | Review note                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --------------------------- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Business                    | (d)         | Revenue above the adjusted threshold ($26,625,000 from 2025-01-01; next adjustment 2027-01-01), or 100,000+ consumers or households bought, sold or shared, or 50%+ of revenue from selling or sharing |
| Personal information        | (v)         | Includes identifiers, online activity, geolocation, inferences and household data; publicly available and deidentified data are excluded                                                                 |
| Sensitive personal information | (ae)     | Government IDs, account log-in with credentials, financial account with access code, precise geolocation, racial or ethnic origin, citizenship or immigration status, religion, union membership, message contents, genetic, neural, biometric for identification, health, sex life or orientation |
| Sell, share                 | (ad), (ah)  | Sale: disclosure to a third party for monetary or other valuable consideration. Sharing: disclosure for cross-context behavioral advertising (k), paid or not; most ad pixels and SDKs fall here          |
| Service provider, contractor | (ag), (j)  | Process for a business purpose under a written contract with the §1798.100(d) and Regs. §7051 terms; without it the recipient is a third party and the disclosure may be a sale or sharing            |
| Consent, dark pattern       | (h), (l)    | Agreement obtained through a dark pattern is not consent                                                                                                                                                 |

## Requirements by topic

| Topic                          | Sections                                      | What to verify                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Minimization and purpose       | §1798.100(c); Regs. §7002                      | Collection, use, retention and sharing reasonably necessary and proportionate to disclosed purposes, or compatible with the consumer's reasonable expectations                                                         |
| Retention                      | §1798.100(a)(3)                               | Retention period per category disclosed, and no longer than reasonably necessary                                                                                                                                    |
| Privacy policy                 | §1798.130(a)(5); Regs. §7011                   | Required content, updated at least every 12 months                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Notice at collection           | §1798.100(a)-(b); Regs. §7012                  | Given at or before collection; without it the business may not collect                                                                                                                                              |
| Know, delete, correct          | §1798.105, .106, .110, .115, .130; Regs. §7020-7024 | Two or more request methods including a toll-free number (email alone for online-only businesses); confirm receipt within 10 business days; respond within 45 calendar days, extendable once by 45 with notice; deletion reaches service providers, contractors and third parties     |
| Opt-out of sale and sharing    | §1798.120, .135; Regs. §7013, 7026            | "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link or the alternative link (Regs. §7015); two or more methods; no verification; honored within 15 business days, with notice to third parties                     |
| Opt-out preference signals     | §1798.135(b), (e); Regs. §7025                 | A signal such as GPC is a valid opt-out for the browser or device and any linked profile; the site displays whether it was honored (§7025(c)(6))                                                                    |
| Limit sensitive information    | §1798.121; Regs. §7014, 7027                   | "Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information" link when sensitive data is used beyond the §7027(m) purposes; honored within 15 business days                                                                   |
| Minors                         | §1798.120(c); Regs. §7070-7072                 | No sale or sharing of consumers under 16 without opt-in: the consumer's own at 13-15, a parent's under 13                                                                                                          |
| Non-discrimination, incentives | §1798.125; Regs. §7016, 7080-7081              | No penalty for exercising rights; financial incentives need a notice and a good-faith value of the data                                                                                                             |
| Request methods, dark patterns | Regs. §7004                                    | Plain language, symmetry in choice, no confusing toggles or manipulative wording, no unnecessary steps                                                                                                              |
| Verification, agents           | Regs. §7060-7063                               | Verification proportionate to the request's risk; authorized agents accepted                                                                                                                                        |
| Contracts                      | §1798.100(d); Regs. §7050-7053                 | Service provider, contractor and third-party contracts with the required terms                                                                                                                                      |
| Training, records              | Regs. §7100-7102                               | Staff handling requests trained; request records kept 24 months; businesses handling 10,000,000+ consumers publish request metrics                                                                                    |
| Security                       | §1798.100(e), §1798.150                        | Reasonable security; a breach of nonencrypted or nonredacted personal information caused by unreasonable security opens a private action ($107-$799 per consumer per incident from 2025)                              |
| Cybersecurity audits           | Regs. §7120-7124                               | Required when 50%+ of revenue comes from selling or sharing, or above the revenue threshold with 250,000+ consumers or 50,000+ consumers' sensitive data; first report due 2028-04-01, 2029-04-01 or 2030-04-01 by revenue tier |
| Risk assessments               | Regs. §7150-7157                               | Before selling or sharing, processing sensitive data, using ADMT for a significant decision, certain workplace, education or sensitive-location inferences, or training ADMT or identification technology; processing begun before 2026 assessed by 2027-12-31; submission to CalPrivacy by 2028-04-01, then yearly |
| ADMT                           | Regs. §7001(e), (ddd), §7200-7222              | Technology that replaces or substantially replaces human decisionmaking for a significant decision (financial or lending, housing, education, employment, healthcare): pre-use notice, opt-out or human appeal, access; compliance from 2027-01-01 |
| Enforcement                    | §1798.155, .199.90                             | Up to $2,663 per violation, $7,988 intentional or involving minors under 16 (2025 amounts); no right to cure                                                                                                         |

## Changes to watch

- AB 566 (California Opt Me Out Act, 2025) requires browsers to offer an opt-out preference signal setting from 2027-01-01; expect more signals to reach the code under review.
- CalPrivacy ran 2026 preliminary rulemaking on opt-out preference signals, notices and disclosures, employee data, and friction in exercising rights. Check the regulations page for packages adopted since 2026-10-28.
- Data brokers register and process deletion requests through the Delete Request and Opt-out Platform (DROP) under the Delete Act; check CalPrivacy for current broker obligations.
