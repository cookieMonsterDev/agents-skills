# Document review checklist

Use this checklist for children's online privacy notices, direct notices to parents, consent screens and scripts, data retention policies, information security programs, vendor and SDK terms, safe harbor membership records, and marketing material that bears on audience.

Check two things for each document: that it contains what the Rule requires, and that it matches what the system actually does. A notice that omits an SDK the app loads, or claims no third-party disclosure while ad SDKs run, is a finding. When both a document and code are in scope, cross-check them against the step 2 inventory.

## Online notice (§312.4(a), (d))

- [ ] Clear, complete, free of unrelated, confusing or contradictory material
- [ ] Prominent, clearly labeled link on the home or landing screen and at each collection point; for a general-audience service with a children's area, on that area's landing screen
- [ ] Name, address, phone and email of every operator collecting or maintaining children's data, or one contact operator plus all operators' names
- [ ] What is collected from children, including whether children can make information public
- [ ] How the information is used
- [ ] Disclosure practices: identities or specific categories of third parties and the purposes
- [ ] The written data retention policy (§312.10)
- [ ] Where persistent identifiers are collected under §312.5(c)(7): the specific internal operations and how the identifier is kept from contacting, profiling or behavioral advertising
- [ ] Where voice audio is collected under §312.5(c)(9): how it is used and that it is deleted immediately after the request
- [ ] Parents' rights to review, delete and refuse further collection, and how to exercise them

## Direct notice to the parent (§312.4(b)-(c))

For consent (§312.4(c)(1)):

- [ ] That contact information was collected from the child to seek consent, if so
- [ ] That consent is required and nothing will be collected, used or disclosed without it
- [ ] The items to be collected, their use and potential disclosures
- [ ] Third parties' identities or categories and purposes, and that the parent can consent to collection without consenting to disclosure unless disclosure is integral
- [ ] Link to the online notice
- [ ] How to give consent
- [ ] That contact details are deleted if consent does not arrive within a reasonable time

For voluntary notice, multiple contacts and child safety, check the elements of §312.4(c)(2), (c)(3) and (c)(4) respectively.

## Consent flow and records (§312.5)

- [ ] The method matches §312.5(b)(2) or a safe-harbor-approved method, with the restrictions of that method (prompt deletion of IDs and images; email plus or text plus only without disclosure)
- [ ] Separate, unbundled consent for third-party disclosure, with refusal leaving the core service available
- [ ] Consent is renewed for material changes
- [ ] Records prove who consented, when, and to what

## Data retention policy (§312.10)

- [ ] Written and implemented
- [ ] Purposes for which children's data is collected
- [ ] Business need for retaining it
- [ ] A deletion timeframe for each category; "indefinitely" or "as long as needed" alone is a finding
- [ ] Secure deletion method
- [ ] Published in the online notice

## Information security program (§312.8)

- [ ] Written program sized to the data's sensitivity and the operator's scale
- [ ] Named coordinator
- [ ] Risk assessment at least annually
- [ ] Safeguards tied to identified risks
- [ ] Regular testing and monitoring
- [ ] Annual evaluation and update
- [ ] Vendor diligence and written security assurances before any release or collection on the operator's behalf

## Vendor, SDK and ad-network terms

- [ ] Each vendor is classified: internal-operations service provider or third party
- [ ] Service providers are restricted from using children's data for any other purpose
- [ ] Ad and analytics partners are told the service is child-directed, or receive the child-directed signal per request
- [ ] Contracts support parental deletion requests and the retention timeframe

## Safe harbor (§312.11)

- [ ] The program is currently FTC-approved and lists the service as certified
- [ ] Annual assessment results and open disciplinary actions are on file
- [ ] Any consent method approved by the program under §312.5(b)(3) is documented

## Audience evidence

Marketing plans, app store listings, ad targeting settings, investor materials and user reviews count toward the directed-to-children analysis (§312.2). Record any representation that the service is for children, or that it reaches them, against a claim of general audience.
