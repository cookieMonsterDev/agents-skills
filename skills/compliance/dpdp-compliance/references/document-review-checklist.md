# Document review checklist

Use this checklist for notices, consent texts and flows, processor contracts, breach, grievance and rights procedures, children's consent flows, DPIAs and audit reports, and policies that describe processing.

Check two things for each document: that it contains what the DPDP Act and Rules require, and that it matches what the system actually does. A notice that describes processing the code does not perform, or omits processing it does, defeats the "fair account" Rule 3 requires. When both a document and code are in scope, cross-check them against the step 2 inventory.

## Notice (s.5; Rule 3)

- [ ] Standalone: understandable without reading other documents (Rule 3(a))
- [ ] Itemised description of the personal data collected
- [ ] Specified purposes, and the goods, services or uses each enables
- [ ] Link and other means to withdraw consent, as easily as it was given
- [ ] Link and other means to exercise rights under the Act
- [ ] How to complain to the Data Protection Board
- [ ] Available in English or any Eighth Schedule language, at the Data Principal's option (s.5(3))
- [ ] For consent obtained before commencement: a notice given as soon as reasonably practicable (s.5(2))

Also check: plain language, no purposes vaguer than the processing ("for business purposes"), and that legitimate-use processing is not dressed up as consent.

## Consent request and flow (s.6)

- [ ] Each purpose is separately accepted; consent is not a condition for unrelated processing or unnecessary data
- [ ] Clear affirmative action; no pre-ticked boxes or consent by continued use
- [ ] No clause waives rights under the Act or other law; such parts are invalid (s.6(2))
- [ ] Withdrawal mechanism described, with its consequences, and processing ceases within a reasonable time after it (s.6(5)-(6))
- [ ] Consent Manager integration, if used, names a Board-registered Consent Manager (s.6(7)-(9))

## Children's and guardians' consent (s.9; Rules 10-12)

- [ ] Age determination step and the route for a parent to identify as an adult (data held, DigiLocker or authorised token)
- [ ] Verifiable parental consent captured before any processing of the child's data
- [ ] Statement that the service does no tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children
- [ ] Any Fourth Schedule exemption relied on is named, with its conditions met
- [ ] Guardian verification for persons with disability (Rule 11)

## Processor contract (s.8(1)-(2), 8(5), 8(7); Rule 6(1)(f))

The Act requires a valid contract but lists no clauses. The contract should evidence:

- [ ] Processing only for the fiduciary's purposes and instructions
- [ ] Reasonable security safeguards matching Rule 6
- [ ] Breach notice to the fiduciary fast enough for intimation without delay and the 72-hour Board report, with a concrete period
- [ ] Erasure on the fiduciary's instruction, on withdrawal or end of purpose (s.8(7)(b)), and log retention of at least 1 year (Rule 8(3))
- [ ] Sub-processor engagement with authorization and flow-down
- [ ] Assistance with rights requests and grievances
- [ ] Hosting locations, and compliance with any s.16 restriction or sectoral localisation rule
- [ ] Audit or information rights for the fiduciary

## Breach procedure (s.8(6); Rule 7)

- [ ] Defines a breach per s.2(u): unauthorised processing, accidental disclosure, loss of access or destruction
- [ ] Every breach is intimated; there is no risk threshold
- [ ] Data Principals informed without delay with the Rule 7(1) content: nature, extent, timing, likely consequences, mitigation, protective steps, contact
- [ ] Board informed without delay, then a detailed report within 72 hours with the Rule 7(2)(b) content, or a written request for more time
- [ ] Parallel CERT-In report within 6 hours where the incident is reportable under the CERT-In Directions (flag for separate review)
- [ ] Processor-to-fiduciary notification path

## Rights and grievance procedure (s.11-14; Rules 9, 14)

- [ ] Published means and identifiers to make a request (Rule 14(1))
- [ ] Access response covers the s.11(1) summary and the list of fiduciaries and processors data was shared with
- [ ] Correction, completion, updating and erasure handled, with erasure unless retention is legally required (s.12(3))
- [ ] Grievance response period published and no longer than 90 days (Rule 14(3))
- [ ] Nomination of another individual on death or incapacity (s.14)
- [ ] DPO or contact person named in every response (Rule 9)

## Significant Data Fiduciary documents (s.10; Rule 13)

- [ ] DPO based in India, responsible to the board, and the grievance contact
- [ ] Independent data auditor appointed
- [ ] DPIA and data audit every 12 months, with significant observations reported to the Board
- [ ] DPIA describes Data Principals' rights, purposes, and the assessment and management of risk to those rights
- [ ] Algorithmic due diligence recorded (Rule 13(3))
- [ ] Localisation of Government-specified data (Rule 13(4))

## Policies and other documents

For research protocols, specifications and policies that describe processing: build the inventory from the text, then check the ground for each purpose, the research and statistics exemption and its Second Schedule standards (s.17(2)(b); Rule 16), necessity, retention, sharing and transfers. Record each silence in the document as an evidence gap.
