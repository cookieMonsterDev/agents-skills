# Document review checklist

Use this checklist for terms and conditions, contact and legal-representative pages, transparency reports, notice forms and decision messages, complaint procedures, ad and recommender disclosures, trader terms, and VLOP risk assessment and audit reports.

Check two things for each document: that it contains what the DSA requires, and that it matches what the service does. Terms that describe a moderation process the tooling does not run, or omit automated tools that it does run, are a finding. When both a document and code are in scope, cross-check them against the step 2 inventory.

## Contact points and representative (Art. 11-13)

- [ ] Single point of contact for authorities, with languages accepted
- [ ] Single point of contact for recipients, offering at least one channel that is not solely automated
- [ ] For providers without an EU establishment: legal representative named, with address, and notified to the DSC of its member state

## Terms and conditions (Art. 14, 23, 27)

- [ ] Restrictions on content and conduct, in clear, plain, user-friendly language
- [ ] Moderation policies, procedures and tools, including algorithmic decision-making and human review
- [ ] Internal complaint handling rules
- [ ] Misuse policy: what leads to suspension, after warning, and for how long (Art. 23(4))
- [ ] Recommender systems: main parameters, their relative importance, and options to change them (Art. 27)
- [ ] A version minors can understand, where the service is primarily directed at or used by minors (14(3))
- [ ] VLOPs: concise summary with remedies, in the official languages of every member state where offered (14(5)-(6))

## Transparency reports (Art. 15, 24, 42; Implementing Regulation 2024/2835)

- [ ] Uses the harmonised templates, with every applicable field filled or justified as not applicable
- [ ] Orders received, by member state and type, with handling times
- [ ] Notices, by category and whether from trusted flaggers, actions taken and median handling time
- [ ] Own-initiative moderation, including automated tools, their purposes, accuracy indicators and safeguards
- [ ] Complaints, their basis, outcome and median handling time
- [ ] Platforms: out-of-court disputes and outcomes, Art. 23 suspensions, average monthly active recipients
- [ ] VLOPs: moderator resources and qualifications per official language, six-monthly cycle
- [ ] Published in a machine-readable, easily accessible format, on time for the period

## Notice form and decision messages (Art. 16, 17, 20, 21)

- [ ] Notice form explains what to provide and confirms receipt
- [ ] Decision messages to notifiers state the outcome, redress, and use of automated means
- [ ] Statements of reasons carry every Art. 17(3) element, specific to the case rather than a generic policy quote
- [ ] Redress text names internal complaints, certified out-of-court bodies, and judicial remedy

## Advertising and recommender disclosures (Art. 26, 27, 39)

- [ ] Ad label wording is clear and prominent, with advertiser, payer and targeting parameters reachable from the ad
- [ ] Policy bans targeting on special categories and profiling-based ads to known minors
- [ ] VLOPs: ad repository documented and publicly reachable

## Marketplace trader terms (Art. 30-32)

- [ ] Trader obligations to supply and keep traceability data accurate, and the consequences of inaccurate data
- [ ] Self-certification text committing to offer only compliant products or services
- [ ] Consumer-facing display of trader identity and contact details

## VLOP risk assessment and audit (Art. 34-37, 41)

- [ ] Risk assessment covers every 34(1) systemic risk and the 34(2) factors: recommender and ad systems, moderation, terms enforcement, data practices
- [ ] Each risk has mitigation measures (35) and an effectiveness measure
- [ ] Assessment done annually and before launching critical-impact functionality, with supporting documents kept for three years
- [ ] Independent audit report and the audit implementation report, with operational recommendations addressed
- [ ] Compliance function independent, with a head reporting to the management body

## Papers and other documents

For product specifications, policies and research that describe a service: classify the tier, then check each triggered obligation the document addresses or should. Record each silence as an evidence gap.
