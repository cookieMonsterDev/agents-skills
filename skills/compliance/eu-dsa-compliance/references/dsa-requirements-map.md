# DSA requirements map

A compact map from provider tiers and review topics to the DSA articles and official guidance behind them. It summarizes; it does not replace the text. Quote or paraphrase the article itself in a finding, and open the source when the exact wording matters.

## Official sources

- [Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act), EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2022/2065/oj): the authoritative text, recitals included. EUR-Lex may show a bot check to scripted requests; open it in a browser.
- [Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2835, EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2024/2835/oj): mandatory templates for Art. 15, 24 and 42 transparency reports, with data collection from 1 July 2025 and the first harmonised reports in early 2026. Check its articles for the current reporting periods and deadlines.
- [Commission: the Digital Services Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/digital-services-act): overview, guidelines and implementing acts.
- [Designated VLOPs and VLOSEs](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/list-designated-vlops-and-vloses): the current designation list.
- [DSA Transparency Database](https://transparency.dsa.ec.europa.eu/): where online platforms submit statements of reasons (Art. 24(5)), with its API documentation.
- [Digital Services Coordinators](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/dsa-dscs): national enforcers, which also certify dispute settlement bodies and award trusted flagger status.

## Obligations by tier

Obligations are cumulative. Applicable to all providers since 17 February 2024.

| Tier                                | Articles      | Obligations                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| All intermediaries                  | 4-10, 11-15   | Liability conditions (4-6) and no general monitoring duty (8); act on authority orders (9, 10); contact point for authorities (11) and recipients (12); legal representative when not established in the EU (13); terms (14); annual report (15) |
| Hosting services                    | 16-18         | Notice and action (16); statement of reasons (17); notify suspected criminal offences threatening life or safety (18)                                                                                                                         |
| Online platforms                    | 19-28         | Complaints (20); out-of-court settlement (21); trusted flaggers (22); misuse measures (23); extra reporting and recipient figures (24); dark patterns (25); ads (26); recommender systems (27); minors (28). Micro and small enterprises: Art. 19 |
| Online marketplaces                 | 29-32         | Trader traceability (30); compliance by design (31); right to information on illegal products (32). Micro and small enterprises: Art. 29                                                                                                       |
| VLOPs and VLOSEs                    | 33-43         | Systemic risk assessment (34) and mitigation (35); crisis response (36); independent audit (37); non-profiling recommender option (38); ad repository (39); data access (40); compliance function (41); six-monthly reports (42); fee (43) |

## Requirements by topic

| Topic                     | Articles          | What to verify                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Points of contact         | 11, 12, 13        | Published, easily accessible contacts; recipients can choose a channel that is not solely automated; legal representative named to a DSC, with mandate and resources, and liable for non-compliance                                                          |
| Terms and conditions      | 14                | Restrictions, moderation policies and tools including algorithmic decision-making and human review, and complaint rules, in clear, plain language; minors' version where the service targets them (14(3)); diligent, objective, proportionate enforcement (14(4)); VLOP summary and languages (14(5)-(6)) |
| Transparency reports      | 15, 24, 42        | Orders, notices, own-initiative moderation, use of automated tools and their accuracy, complaints; platforms add disputes, suspensions and figures; VLOPs every six months with moderator resources per language; Implementing Regulation 2024/2835 templates |
| Recipient figures         | 24(2), 24(3)      | Average monthly active recipients in the EU published at least every six months, method per Recital 77; given to the DSC or Commission on request                                                                                                            |
| Notice and action         | 16                | Electronic, user-friendly notice form collecting the explanation, exact location (URL), notifier name and email (except for listed child sexual abuse offences), and a good-faith statement; acknowledgement; timely, diligent, non-arbitrary decision; notifier told of the decision and of any automated means |
| Statement of reasons      | 17, 24(5)         | Clear, specific reasons to the affected recipient for removal, demotion, demonetisation, suspension or account restriction, with the 17(3) content: restriction, facts, automated means, legal or contractual ground, redress; platforms submit it to the Transparency Database without personal data |
| Criminal offences         | 18                | Promptly inform law enforcement of information giving rise to suspicion of an offence threatening life or safety                                                                                                                                              |
| Internal complaints       | 20                | Free, electronic, for at least six months after the decision; decided under qualified staff supervision, not solely by automated means; decision reversed without undue delay when justified                                                                     |
| Out-of-court settlement   | 21                | Recipients informed that they can use a certified body; engagement in good faith; fees borne per 21(5)                                                                                                                                                        |
| Trusted flaggers          | 22                | Their notices processed with priority and without undue delay                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Misuse                    | 23                | Suspension, after a prior warning, of recipients frequently providing manifestly illegal content and of frequent manifestly unfounded notifiers; policy stated in the terms                                                                                    |
| Dark patterns             | 25                | No interface that deceives, manipulates or impairs free decisions, e.g. prominence of one choice, repeated prompts, harder cancellation than sign-up; outside practices already covered by the UCPD or GDPR (25(2))                                              |
| Advertising               | 26                | Per ad, in real time: marked as an ad, on whose behalf, who paid, main targeting parameters and how to change them; users can declare commercial communications; no profiling-based ads using GDPR Art. 9 special categories (26(3))                         |
| Recommender systems       | 27, 38            | Main parameters and how to change them in the terms; selection functionality accessible where options exist; VLOPs offer at least one option not based on profiling                                                                                          |
| Minors                    | 28                | Appropriate and proportionate measures for a high level of privacy, safety and security (28(1), Commission guidelines of July 2025); no profiling-based ads when the platform is aware with reasonable certainty the recipient is a minor (28(2)); no duty to collect extra data to assess age (28(3)) |
| Trader traceability       | 30                | Before allowing traders: name, address, phone and email; identification document; payment account; trade register number; self-certification of compliant products; best-efforts verification; suspend on inaccurate data; keep for six months after the relationship ends |
| Compliance by design      | 31                | Interface lets traders give pre-contractual, compliance and product safety information; best-efforts random checks against official databases                                                                                                                 |
| Illegal products          | 32                | Inform consumers who bought an illegal product or service through the platform in the past six months                                                                                                                                                        |
| Systemic risks (VLOPs)    | 34, 35, 37        | Annual risk assessment, and before launching functionality likely to have critical impact, covering illegal content, fundamental rights, civic discourse and elections, public security, gender-based violence, public health, minors and well-being; mitigation; annual independent audit |
| Ad repository (VLOPs)     | 39                | Searchable repository with ad content, advertiser, payer, period, targeting parameters and reach, kept for one year after the ad was last shown, without personal data of recipients                                                                         |
| Data access (VLOPs)       | 40                | Access for the DSC and Commission, and for vetted researchers under the Commission's delegated act on data access; check the live source for its procedures                                                                                                  |
| Penalties                 | 52, 74, 76        | Up to 6% of worldwide annual turnover; up to 1% for incorrect, incomplete or misleading information; periodic penalties up to 5% of average daily turnover                                                                                                     |

## Guidance worth opening

- [Guidelines on the protection of minors under Art. 28](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/commission-publishes-guidelines-protection-minors): age assurance, default settings, recommender systems and commercial practices; the Commission's benchmark for Art. 28(1).
- [Harmonised transparency reporting rules](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-harmonises-transparency-reporting-rules-under-digital-services-act): templates and instructions for the implementing regulation.
- [Trusted flaggers under the DSA](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/trusted-flaggers-under-dsa) and [out-of-court dispute settlement bodies](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/dsa-out-court-dispute-settlement): current designations.
- [EDPB Guidelines 3/2025 on the interplay between the DSA and the GDPR](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/our-work-tools/documents/public-consultations/2025/guidelines-32025-interplay-between-dsa-and-gdpr_en): personal data in notice and action, ads, recommender systems and minors' protection.
