# Document review checklist

Use this checklist for business associate agreements, notices of privacy practices, authorizations, risk analyses, security policies, breach and access procedures, HDS hosting contracts and certificates, and papers that describe health data processing.

Check two things for each document: that it contains what the rule requires, and that it matches what the system actually does. When both a document and code are in scope, cross-check them against the step 2 inventory.

## Business associate agreement (164.504(e), 164.314(a))

- [ ] Permitted and required uses and disclosures; no use the covered entity could not make itself
- [ ] Appropriate safeguards and Security Rule compliance for ePHI
- [ ] Report any use or disclosure outside the agreement, security incidents and breaches of unsecured PHI, with a concrete deadline within 60 days
- [ ] Subcontractors bound by the same restrictions through their own BAAs
- [ ] Make PHI available for access, amendment and accounting of disclosures
- [ ] Books and records available to HHS
- [ ] Return or destroy PHI at termination, or extend protections where infeasible
- [ ] Termination right on material breach

Also check: de-identification, data aggregation and AI training rights are stated or excluded; offshore processing is addressed.

## Notice of privacy practices (164.520)

- [ ] Required header statement, uses and disclosures with examples, and those needing authorization
- [ ] Individual rights and how to exercise them
- [ ] Covered entity's duties, complaint process to the entity and HHS, contact, effective date
- [ ] Posted, distributed and acknowledged as the rule requires; updated for material changes. Check the live status of the 2024 amendments (Part 2 and reproductive health) after the June 2025 vacatur of the reproductive health rule.

## Authorization (164.508)

- [ ] Specific description of the information, recipients, purpose and expiry
- [ ] Revocation right, conditioning statement, redisclosure statement, signature and date
- [ ] Marketing with remuneration and sale of PHI disclosed in the authorization

## Risk analysis and risk management (164.308(a)(1))

- [ ] Covers every system, location and vendor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits ePHI
- [ ] Threats, vulnerabilities, likelihood, impact and risk level for each
- [ ] Risk management plan with owners and dates; addressable decisions documented with reasons
- [ ] Updated after environment changes and reviewed periodically
- [ ] Policies kept 6 years (164.316(b))

## Breach procedure (164.400-164.414)

- [ ] Discovery defined, including when a workforce member or agent knew or should have known
- [ ] Four-factor risk assessment: nature and extent of PHI, who received it, whether it was acquired or viewed, mitigation
- [ ] Notice to individuals within 60 days with the 164.404(c) content
- [ ] HHS and media notice by the 500 threshold; yearly log for smaller breaches
- [ ] Business associate path to the covered entity; law enforcement delay handled
- [ ] Records kept to meet the burden of proof (164.414)

## Access and amendment procedure (164.524, 164.526)

- [ ] Access within 30 days, one 30-day extension with notice; electronic copy in the form requested when readily producible
- [ ] Fee limited to reasonable, cost-based amounts
- [ ] Denial grounds and review rights; amendment within 60 days

## HDS hosting contract (L.1111-8; R.1111-11)

- [ ] Hosting activities covered and the host's HDS certificate referenced, valid and covering them
- [ ] Data localisation in the EEA, sub-hosts included
- [ ] Transfers outside the EEA, remote access included, with safeguards and supplementary measures where no adequacy decision exists
- [ ] Disclosure of the host's or its chain's exposure to non-EEA law
- [ ] Sub-hosts named, with prior information or authorization
- [ ] Service levels, security incident reporting to the client, audit rights
- [ ] Reversibility: export format, timelines and deletion at end of contract
- [ ] Clauses required by Décret n° 2026-209; check the consolidated R.1111-11 on Légifrance

## HDS certificate

- [ ] Issued by an accredited body against referential v2 (or v2.1 once applicable)
- [ ] Lists the activities the host actually performs for this client
- [ ] In date, with surveillance audits current; host appears on the ANS list of certified hosts

## Papers and other documents

For research papers, protocols, proposals and specifications: build the inventory from the text, then check the HIPAA research pathway (authorization, IRB or privacy board waiver, limited data set, or de-identification), minimum necessary, hosting and its HDS status, transfers, retention, and GDPR or CNIL research rules for French data. Record each silence in the document as an evidence gap.
