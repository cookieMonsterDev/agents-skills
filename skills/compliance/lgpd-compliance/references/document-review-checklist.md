# Document review checklist

Use this checklist for privacy notices, consent and cookie texts, operator contracts, records of processing, RIPDs, incident and data subject request procedures, transfer documentation, and policies that describe processing.

Check two things for each document: that it contains what the LGPD and ANPD rules require, and that it matches what the system actually does. A complete notice that describes processing the code does not perform, or omits processing it does, is a transparency finding (Art. 6(VI)). When both a document and code are in scope, cross-check them against the step 2 inventory. Documents for Brazilian data subjects should be available in Portuguese.

## Privacy notice (Art. 6(VI), 9, 18, 41)

- [ ] Controller identity and contact details
- [ ] Encarregado identity and contact details, published clearly and preferably on the website (Art. 41 §1), or the small-agent channel
- [ ] Specific purpose of each processing activity and its legal basis
- [ ] Form and duration of processing, including retention
- [ ] Shared use: recipients or categories of recipients and the purpose of sharing (Art. 9(V), 18(VII))
- [ ] International transfers: destination, mechanism, and the Res. 19/2024 transparency information
- [ ] Data subject rights under Art. 18 and how to exercise them, including the right to petition the ANPD (18 §1)
- [ ] Where consent is the basis: the possibility of refusing and its consequences, and how to revoke (18(VIII)-(IX))
- [ ] Automated decisions and the right to request their review (Art. 20)
- [ ] Legitimate interest named where relied on, with the interest described (Art. 10 §2)

Also check: clear and accessible language, no generic purposes ("to improve our services" alone), and age-appropriate wording where children are users (Art. 14 §6).

## Consent and cookie texts (Art. 8, 11(I), 14 §1)

- [ ] Each purpose can be accepted or refused separately; consent is not bundled into the terms of use
- [ ] Consent clauses stand out from other contractual terms when given in writing (Art. 8 §1)
- [ ] Sensitive-data consent is specific and highlighted
- [ ] Children's data: specific and highlighted consent by a parent or guardian where consent is the basis
- [ ] Revocation is free and simple, and the text says how (Art. 8 §5)
- [ ] Cookie list states purpose, provider and duration; necessary cookies are separated from the rest (ANPD cookie guide)
- [ ] A change of purpose or of the Art. 9 information is announced in advance, with the option to revoke consent (Art. 8 §6, 9 §2)

## Operator contract (Art. 37-42, 46)

The LGPD lists no fixed clauses, but the contract should evidence:

- [ ] Processing only on the controller's documented instructions (Art. 39)
- [ ] Confidentiality of everyone involved in processing (Art. 47)
- [ ] Security measures matching Art. 46 and any sector rules
- [ ] Sub-operator engagement only with authorization, and equivalent obligations flowed down
- [ ] Assistance with data subject requests, incident communication and RIPDs
- [ ] Incident notice to the controller fast enough for its 3-business-day deadline, with a concrete period
- [ ] Deletion or return of data at the end of services
- [ ] Records of processing kept by the operator (Art. 37) and audit or information rights
- [ ] International transfers covered by the ANPD standard clauses in full, unaltered, or another Art. 33 mechanism
- [ ] Liability terms that leave the Art. 42 joint liability rules intact

## Records of processing (Art. 37; Res. 2/2022, Art. 9)

- [ ] Every processing activity, with purpose, legal basis, data categories, data subjects, recipients, transfers, retention and security measures
- [ ] Kept by both controller and operator; small agents may use the ANPD's simplified template
- [ ] Cross-checked against the inventory; missing activities are the usual finding

## RIPD (Art. 5(XVII), 10 §3, 38)

- [ ] Description of the types of data collected, the methodology of collection and of security
- [ ] Necessity and proportionality of the processing
- [ ] Risks to data subjects' civil liberties and fundamental rights, not only to the business
- [ ] Measures, safeguards and risk mitigation mechanisms, with residual risk
- [ ] Encarregado involvement and a review date or trigger
- [ ] Exists for legitimate-interest processing and for high-risk processing (Res. 2/2022, Art. 4 criteria), ready to provide when the ANPD requests it

## Incident procedure (Art. 48; Res. 15/2024)

- [ ] Defines a security incident (confidentiality, integrity, availability, authenticity) and who decides on communication
- [ ] Risk assessment uses the Res. 15 Art. 5 criteria: significant effect on rights plus sensitive, children's, adolescents' or elderly people's, financial, authentication, secrecy-protected or large-scale data
- [ ] ANPD and affected data subjects communicated within 3 business days of knowing personal data was affected; double for small agents
- [ ] Communication content per Res. 15 Art. 6 §2 and Art. 9; supplements within 20 business days
- [ ] Declaration to the ANPD that data subjects were informed, within 3 business days after that deadline (Art. 9 §4)
- [ ] Operator-to-controller notification path
- [ ] Incident record kept for at least 5 years, including uncommunicated incidents (Art. 10)

## Data subject request procedure (Art. 18-20)

- [ ] Intake through accessible channels, with proportionate identity verification
- [ ] Confirmation and access: simplified immediately, or complete within 15 days (Art. 19); double for small agents
- [ ] Free of charge (Art. 18 §5)
- [ ] Corrections and deletions passed on to the agents data was shared with (Art. 18 §6)
- [ ] Where a request cannot be met immediately, the reply gives the reasons or names the agent who holds the data (Art. 18 §4)
- [ ] Review of automated decisions on request, with information on criteria, subject to trade secrets (Art. 20)

## International transfer documentation (Art. 33; Res. 19/2024)

- [ ] Each transfer has a mechanism and a legal basis under Art. 7 or 11
- [ ] ANPD standard contractual clauses incorporated in full and unaltered, filling only the marked fields (Res. 19 Art. 16 and Annex II)
- [ ] Other contract terms do not contradict the clauses
- [ ] Transparency page in Portuguese describing the transfers, and the full clauses provided on request within 15 days (Res. 19 Art. 17)
- [ ] Reliance on adequacy matches a current ANPD adequacy decision for the destination

## Policies and other documents

For research protocols, specifications and policies that describe processing: build the inventory from the text, then check legal basis, research-body conditions (Art. 7(IV), 11(II)(c), 13), necessity and pseudonymisation, information given to participants, retention, sharing and transfers, and whether a RIPD is warranted. Record each silence in the document as an evidence gap.
