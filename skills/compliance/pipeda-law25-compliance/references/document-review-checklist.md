# Document review checklist

Use this checklist for privacy policies, consent texts, PIAs, confidentiality incident procedures and registers, service contracts, access-request procedures, governance policies, and papers that describe processing.

Check two things for each document: that it contains what PIPEDA and Law 25 require, and that it matches what the system actually does. A complete policy that describes processing the code does not perform, or omits processing it does, is an openness finding. When both a document and code are in scope, cross-check them against the step 2 inventory. Québec documents for the public are usually needed in French under the Charter of the French language; flag a missing French version for counsel.

## Privacy policy and collection notice (Principles 4.2, 4.8; Québec s. 3.2, 8, 8.1, 8.2)

- [ ] Title and contact details of the person in charge of protecting personal information (Québec s. 3.1; Principle 4.1)
- [ ] Purposes of collection, specific enough to understand ("to improve our services" alone is too vague)
- [ ] Means of collection, including technological means such as cookies and SDKs (Québec s. 8, 8.2)
- [ ] Categories of persons within the enterprise with access, and third parties or categories receiving the information
- [ ] Whether information may be communicated outside Québec, and use of service providers outside Canada (OPC cross-border guidelines)
- [ ] Use of technology that identifies, locates or profiles, and how to activate it (Québec s. 8.1)
- [ ] Rights of access, rectification, portability, withdrawal of consent, de-indexation, and how to exercise them
- [ ] Use of exclusively automated decisions, where applicable (Québec s. 12.1)
- [ ] Retention periods or criteria, and the complaint procedure (Principle 4.10; Québec s. 3.2)
- [ ] Published on the website in clear and simple terms, with notice of changes (Québec s. 3.2, 8.2)

## Consent texts (PIPEDA s. 6.1; Principle 4.3; Québec s. 4.1, 12, 14)

- [ ] Each purpose requested separately, apart from other terms (Québec s. 14)
- [ ] The key elements the OPC guidelines emphasize: what information, for what purposes, shared with whom, and the risk of harm
- [ ] Express consent for sensitive information and for uses outside reasonable expectations
- [ ] Consent is not a condition of service beyond what the service needs (Principle 4.3.3)
- [ ] Withdrawal explained, with its consequences (Principle 4.3.8)
- [ ] Parent or tutor consent for minors under 14 (Québec s. 4.1); language a minor can understand where minors are addressed
- [ ] Consent interfaces present choices neutrally, with refusing as easy as accepting

## Privacy impact assessment (Québec s. 3.3, 17, 21)

- [ ] Triggered for each project to acquire, develop or overhaul an information system or electronic service delivery, and before communicating outside Québec
- [ ] Proportionate to sensitivity, purpose, quantity, distribution and medium of the information
- [ ] For outside-Québec communication: sensitivity, purposes, protection measures including contractual ones, and the destination's legal framework and its privacy principles (s. 17)
- [ ] Conclusion that the information would receive adequate protection, and a written agreement reflecting the PIA (s. 17)
- [ ] Person in charge consulted from the start of the project (s. 3.3)
- [ ] Portability of computerized information designed in (s. 3.3)

## Service provider contract (Principle 4.1.3; Québec s. 18.3)

- [ ] Written mandate or contract stating the protection measures
- [ ] Use limited to performing the mandate, with no retention after it ends
- [ ] Notice to the enterprise of any violation or attempted violation of confidentiality
- [ ] Right for the enterprise to verify compliance
- [ ] Sub-processing and cross-border hosting addressed, consistent with the s. 17 PIA

## Breach and incident procedure (PIPEDA s. 10.1-10.3; Québec s. 3.5-3.8)

- [ ] Defines a confidentiality incident (unauthorized access, use, communication or loss) and a breach of security safeguards
- [ ] Immediate steps to reduce the risk and prevent recurrence (Québec s. 3.5)
- [ ] Federal test: real risk of significant harm, from sensitivity and probability of misuse; report to the OPC and notify individuals as soon as feasible
- [ ] Québec test: risk of serious injury, assessed with the person in charge; notify the CAI and the persons concerned promptly
- [ ] Notice contents follow the federal Regulations (s. 2-3) and the Québec incident regulation
- [ ] Other organizations that can reduce harm are notified (PIPEDA s. 10.2)
- [ ] One record or register covers every incident: federal record kept 24 months; Québec register content per the incident regulation, available to the CAI on request

## Access and rights procedure (Principle 4.9; PIPEDA s. 8; Québec s. 27-32)

- [ ] Written requests accepted, with help for the requester; proportionate identity verification
- [ ] Reply within 30 days; PIPEDA extension by up to 30 more days with notice and reasons
- [ ] Portability format and transfer to a designated body (Québec s. 27)
- [ ] Refusals give reasons and the recourse: OPC complaint or CAI review
- [ ] De-indexation requests assessed against the s. 28.1 criteria

## Governance policies (Principle 4.1; Québec s. 3.1, 3.2)

- [ ] Person in charge designated, with delegation in writing where it is not the highest-ranking person
- [ ] Retention and destruction rules, staff roles and responsibilities, complaint handling
- [ ] Training and awareness for staff with access

## Papers and other documents

For research papers, proposals, specifications and policies that describe processing: build the inventory from the text, then check purposes, consent or the research exceptions (PIPEDA s. 7(2)(c), 7(3)(f); Québec s. 21-21.0.2 with a PIA), minimization and de-identification, retention, sharing and communication outside Québec or Canada. Record each silence in the document as an evidence gap.
