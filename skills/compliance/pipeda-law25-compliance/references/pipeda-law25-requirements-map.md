# PIPEDA and Law 25 requirements map

A compact map from review topics to PIPEDA (S.C. 2000, c. 5, Part 1 and Schedule 1) and the Québec Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (CQLR c. P-39.1, "Québec s."), as amended by Law 25 (S.Q. 2021, c. 25, formerly Bill 64). It summarizes; it does not replace the text. Quote or paraphrase the section itself in a finding, and open the source when the exact wording matters.

## Official sources

- [PIPEDA, Justice Laws](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-8.6/): the consolidated Act; the ten fair information principles are in Schedule 1.
- [Breach of Security Safeguards Regulations (SOR/2018-64)](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-64/): report and notice content, and the 24-month record.
- [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC)](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/): guidance, findings and the breach report form.
- Québec private sector act, LégisQuébec: the consolidated Act as amended by Law 25. Search LégisQuébec for chapter P-39.1, or use the CanLII copy.
- [Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (CAI)](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/): the Québec regulator, its guides (PIA, consent, incidents) and the incident notification form.
- [Bill C-36, LEGISinfo](https://www.parl.ca/LegisInfo/en/bill/45-1/C-36): the pending federal reform that would replace PIPEDA Part 1; check its stage before citing it.

Law 25 took effect in three stages: 22 September 2022 (person in charge, confidentiality incidents), 22 September 2023 (most obligations) and 22 September 2024 (portability). Regulations made under it: the Regulation respecting confidentiality incidents and the Regulation respecting the anonymization of personal information.

## PIPEDA: the ten principles (Schedule 1)

| Principle                                      | Clause | What to verify                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Accountability                                 | 4.1    | A designated person; policies and practices; contractual protection when a third party processes information (4.1.3)                 |
| Identifying purposes                           | 4.2    | Purposes documented and stated at or before collection; new purposes identified before use                                           |
| Consent                                        | 4.3    | Knowledge and consent for collection, use and disclosure; express consent for sensitive information; withdrawal (4.3.8); s. 6.1 validity |
| Limiting collection                            | 4.4    | Only what the identified purposes need, by fair and lawful means                                                                     |
| Limiting use, disclosure and retention         | 4.5    | Use only for identified purposes; retention schedule; destroy, erase or anonymize when no longer needed                              |
| Accuracy                                       | 4.6    | Accurate, complete and up to date enough for the purpose                                                                             |
| Safeguards                                     | 4.7    | Physical, organizational and technological measures proportionate to sensitivity; staff awareness                                    |
| Openness                                       | 4.8    | Policies and practices readily available in understandable form                                                                     |
| Individual access                              | 4.9    | Access and correction; reply within 30 days, extendable by 30 with notice (s. 8(3)-(4)); exceptions in s. 9                          |
| Challenging compliance                         | 4.10   | A complaint procedure that is accessible and simple                                                                                  |

Also: s. 5(3) appropriate purposes (what a reasonable person would consider appropriate, regardless of consent); s. 7 exceptions to consent; s. 10.1 breach reporting; s. 10.3 breach records; s. 28 fines up to $100,000 for knowingly failing to report or record a breach.

## PIPEDA: breaches of security safeguards

- **Threshold:** report to the OPC and notify affected individuals as soon as feasible when the breach creates a real risk of significant harm (RROSH), assessed from the sensitivity of the information and the probability of misuse (s. 10.1(1), (3), (7)-(8)).
- **Third parties:** notify other organizations or government institutions that can reduce the risk (s. 10.2).
- **Records:** keep a record of every breach, reportable or not, for 24 months after the determination, available to the OPC on request (s. 10.3; Regulations s. 6).

## Québec: Law 25 by topic

| Topic                              | Québec s.         | What to verify                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Person in charge                   | 3.1               | The highest-ranking person by default, delegable in writing; title and contact details published on the website                                                |
| Governance policies                | 3.2               | Retention and destruction, roles, complaint handling; published in clear terms on the website                                                                    |
| Privacy impact assessment          | 3.3, 17, 21       | PIA for any project to acquire, develop or overhaul an information system or electronic service delivery involving personal information; before communicating outside Québec (17); for research (21) |
| Confidentiality incidents          | 3.5-3.8           | Reduce the risk; notify the CAI and affected persons promptly when there is a risk of serious injury (3.5-3.6); keep a register of all incidents and give it to the CAI on request (3.8) |
| Information at collection          | 8                 | Purposes, means, rights, recipient categories, possibility of communication outside Québec                                                                      |
| Identify, locate or profile        | 8.1               | Inform the person; such functions are off by default and activated by the person                                                                                 |
| Privacy policy                     | 8.2               | Published in clear and simple terms when collecting through technological means                                                                                  |
| Privacy by default                 | 9.1               | Highest confidentiality settings by default for a technological product or service, without intervention; browser cookies excluded                             |
| Consent                            | 4.1, 12, 14       | Manifest, free, enlightened, given for specific purposes, requested separately per purpose, in clear and simple terms (14); express for sensitive information (12); parent or tutor under 14 (4.1) |
| Automated decisions                | 12.1              | Inform the person when a decision is based exclusively on automated processing; on request, the information used, the reasons and principal factors, correction, and a chance to submit observations to a staff member who can review |
| Service providers                  | 18.3              | Written mandate or contract with protection measures, use limited to the mandate, incident notice, and destruction at end                                       |
| Retention and anonymization        | 23                | Destroy or anonymize when the purpose is achieved; anonymize per the regulation's criteria                                                                       |
| Access and portability             | 27, 32            | Access and rectification; computerized information collected from the person in a structured, commonly used technological format, to the person or a body they designate; reply within 30 days |
| De-indexation                      | 28.1              | Cease dissemination or de-index a hyperlink where dissemination contravenes the law or a court order, or causes serious injury to reputation or privacy           |
| Penalties                          | 90.1, 90.12, 91   | Administrative monetary penalties up to $10 million or 2% of worldwide turnover; penal fines up to $25 million or 4%; punitive damages for intentional or gross fault (93.1) |

## OPC guidance worth opening

- [Guidelines for obtaining meaningful consent](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-for-businesses/appropriate-handling-of-personal-information/collecting-personal-information-and-consent/consent/gl_omc_201805/): seven principles, the key elements to emphasize, and when express consent is required.
- [Guidance on inappropriate data practices (s. 5(3))](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-for-businesses/appropriate-handling-of-personal-information/collecting-personal-information-and-consent/consent/gd_53_201805/): the "no-go zones" consent cannot cure.
- [What you need to know about mandatory reporting of breaches](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-for-businesses/privacy-breaches-at-your-business/gd_pb_201810/): RROSH factors, report content and record keeping.
- [Guidelines for processing personal data across borders](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/airports-and-borders/gl_dab_090127/): a transfer for processing is a use, not a disclosure; it needs comparable protection by contract and notice to individuals.

Penalty amounts, CAI guides and the reform bill change: check the live source rather than relying on memory.
