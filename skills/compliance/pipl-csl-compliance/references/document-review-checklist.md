# Document review checklist

Use this checklist for privacy policies (个人信息处理规则), consent texts and pop-ups, SDK and sharing lists, entrustment agreements, standard contracts and filing packs, PI protection impact assessments (PIPIAs), compliance audit reports, incident and rights-request procedures, and papers that describe processing.

Check two things for each document: that it contains what the PIPL requires, and that it matches what the system actually does. When both a document and code are in scope, cross-check them against the step 2 inventory. Review the Chinese version; it governs where translations differ.

## Privacy policy (PIPL Art. 17, 18, 30; NDSMR 21)

- [ ] Processor name and contact details; PRC representative for offshore processors (Art. 53)
- [ ] Purposes, processing methods and PI categories, purpose by purpose
- [ ] Retention period for each category
- [ ] How to exercise each right, and the response channel
- [ ] Sensitive PI: the necessity and the impact on the individual (Art. 30)
- [ ] Recipients and third-party SDKs listed with name, purpose, method and categories
- [ ] Cross-border transfers: recipient name and contact, purpose, method, categories and how to exercise rights against the recipient (Art. 39)
- [ ] Automated decision-making and how to opt out of personalization
- [ ] A separate policy for minors under 14 where they may use the service
- [ ] Prominent, easy to reach and plainly worded; changes notified to users

## Consent texts (PIPL Art. 14-16, 29, 31, 39)

- [ ] Each separate consent has its own explicit act: sharing with another processor, public disclosure, sensitive PI, cross-border transfer, non-security use of public-camera images
- [ ] No pre-ticked boxes; refusing optional processing keeps the core service available
- [ ] Withdrawal explained and as convenient as consenting
- [ ] Guardian consent flow for minors under 14
- [ ] Re-consent triggered by a change of purpose, method or categories

## Entrustment agreement (PIPL Art. 21, 59)

- [ ] Purpose, term, processing method, PI categories and protection measures
- [ ] Rights and duties of both parties; processor supervision and audit rights
- [ ] Return or deletion at the end of the engagement
- [ ] Sub-entrustment only with the processor's consent
- [ ] Security incident notice to the processor

## Cross-border transfer pack (PIPL Art. 38-40; CBDF Provisions)

- [ ] Mechanism matches the volume tier and CIIO status, or the exemption relied on is stated with its facts
- [ ] Standard contract uses the CAC template unaltered, with additional terms only where they do not conflict; filed with the provincial CAC within 10 working days of taking effect
- [ ] Certification: an accredited body's certificate in force and covering the transfer
- [ ] Security assessment: approval in force (3 years) and matching the current purpose, scope and recipients
- [ ] PIPIA completed before the transfer, covering the recipient's country's laws and the recipient's protection level
- [ ] Re-filing or re-assessment triggered by changes in recipients, purposes, categories or the recipient's legal environment

## PIPIA (PIPL Art. 55, 56)

- [ ] Triggered for each Art. 55 scenario in scope
- [ ] Assesses legality, legitimacy and necessity of the purpose and method
- [ ] Impact on individuals' rights and the security risk
- [ ] Protection measures, and whether they are lawful, effective and proportionate to the risk
- [ ] Report and processing record kept at least 3 years

## Compliance audit (PIPL Art. 54; audit measures)

- [ ] Cycle matches volume: at least every 2 years above 10 million individuals
- [ ] Scope follows the annexed audit guideline
- [ ] Independent auditor where CAC orders the audit, and no auditor runs more than three consecutive audits of the same processor
- [ ] Findings tracked to remediation

## Incident procedure (PIPL Art. 57; CSL 27; NDSMR 11; incident reporting measures)

- [ ] Grades incidents using the incident reporting measures' grading criteria
- [ ] Report deadlines set per grade and per operator type (CIIOs within 1 hour)
- [ ] Notice to individuals covers categories, cause, possible harm, remedies, steps they can take and contacts
- [ ] Entrusted parties report to the processor promptly
- [ ] Emergency plan rehearsed; logs and evidence preserved

## Rights-request procedure (PIPL Art. 44-50; NDSMR 23-25)

- [ ] Intake channel convenient and published; identity verified proportionately
- [ ] Response timeline set and met; refusals give reasons and the right to sue
- [ ] Covers access and copy, correction, deletion, portability, explanation, and account cancellation
- [ ] Requests reach entrusted parties and recipients

## Papers and other documents

For research papers, proposals, specifications and policies: build the inventory from the text, then check lawful basis, consent or notice given to participants, sensitive PI handling, de-identification versus anonymization, retention, offshore collaborators and transfers, and whether a PIPIA is needed. Record each silence in the document as an evidence gap.
