# Document review checklist

Use this checklist for privacy notices, cookie banners and policies, marketing consents, data processing agreements, IDTAs and Addendums, transfer risk assessments, records of processing, DPIAs, breach, subject access and complaint procedures, policies, and papers that describe processing.

Check two things for each document: that it contains what UK law requires, and that it matches what the system actually does. A complete notice that describes processing the code does not perform, or omits processing it does, is a transparency finding. When both a document and code are in scope, cross-check them against the step 2 inventory. Where the [`gdpr-compliance` document checklist](../../gdpr-compliance/references/document-review-checklist.md) covers the same item, its list applies with UK GDPR numbering; this file adds the UK differences.

## Privacy notice (Art. 12-14)

- [ ] Everything the EU GDPR Art. 13 and 14 lists require, with "UK" in place of "EU" for representatives, transfers and the regulator
- [ ] UK representative's details for a non-UK controller in Art. 3(2) scope (Art. 27)
- [ ] Each recognised legitimate interest relied on (Art. 6(1)(ea)), and the legitimate interests pursued under Art. 6(1)(f)
- [ ] Restricted transfers outside the UK and the safeguard relied on
- [ ] Automated decisions: that they are made, and the Art. 22C safeguards available
- [ ] The right to complain to the controller (DPA 2018 s. 164A) and to the Information Commission
- [ ] Further processing for a new purpose notified before it starts (Art. 13(3), 14(4))

## Cookie banner and cookie policy (PECR reg. 6, Schedule A1)

- [ ] Consent sought for every non-exempt purpose, before the storage or access happens
- [ ] Analytics and functionality cookies relying on the Schedule A1 exceptions are described with their purpose, and the banner or policy offers a free, simple way to object
- [ ] Reject is as prominent and easy as accept; pre-ticked boxes and continued browsing are not consent
- [ ] Cookie list states purpose, provider, duration and the exception or consent each relies on

## Marketing consents (PECR reg. 21-22)

- [ ] Consent wording names the channels (email, SMS, phone) and the sender, with an unticked box per channel
- [ ] Soft opt-in conditions met: details collected in a sale or negotiation (or, for charities, an expression of interest in their purposes), similar products or aims, and an opt-out at collection and in every message
- [ ] Opt-out mechanism described and free of charge

## Processor contract (Art. 28)

- [ ] The Art. 28(3) terms (instructions, confidentiality, security, sub-processors, assistance, deletion or return, audits)
- [ ] Transfers by the processor covered by a UK transfer mechanism

## Transfer documents (Chapter 5)

- [ ] Mechanism matches the flow: UK adequacy regulations, UK-US data bridge with the recipient's certification, IDTA, or the Addendum attached to the right EU SCC module
- [ ] IDTA or Addendum version is the current ICO-issued one, with tables completed
- [ ] Transfer risk assessment applies the data protection test (protection not materially lower than in the UK)
- [ ] Onward transfers and sub-processors covered

## Records of processing (Art. 30)

- [ ] Required content, including recipients in third countries and the safeguards
- [ ] Covers every activity in the inventory; the under-250 exemption does not cover risky, non-occasional or special category processing

## DPIA (Art. 35)

- [ ] Systematic description, necessity and proportionality, risks to individuals, measures and residual risk
- [ ] ICO's list of processing likely to result in high risk considered
- [ ] DPO advice recorded; prior consultation with the regulator when high residual risk remains (Art. 36)

## Breach procedure (Art. 33, 34)

- [ ] Report to the regulator within 72 hours of awareness unless unlikely to result in risk; tell individuals without undue delay when high risk
- [ ] Notification content per Art. 33(3), allowing information in phases
- [ ] Breach log for every breach (Art. 33(5))

## Subject access procedure (Art. 12, 12A, 15)

- [ ] One month from receipt, identity confirmation or fee; pause while awaiting clarification that is reasonably needed; extension by two months with reasons
- [ ] Search approach documented and reasonable and proportionate (Art. 15(1A))
- [ ] Exemptions under DPA 2018 Schedules 2-4 applied case by case, with reasons recorded

## Complaints procedure (DPA 2018 s. 164A)

- [ ] A way to complain, such as an electronic form, with no barrier to using it
- [ ] Acknowledgement within 30 days of receipt
- [ ] Investigation without undue delay, progress updates, and the outcome communicated
- [ ] Separate from, and pointing to, the route to the regulator

## Accountability items

- [ ] Data protection fee paid, or exemption recorded (Data Protection (Charges and Information) Regulations 2018)
- [ ] Appropriate policy document for Schedule 1 conditions that require one (DPA 2018 Sch. 1 Part 4)
- [ ] DPO designated where Art. 37 requires one

## Papers and other documents

For research papers, proposals, specifications and policies that describe processing: build the inventory from the text, then check lawful basis, the research definitions, broad research consent and safeguards as amended by DUAA s. 67-68 and 86, minimisation and pseudonymisation, information given to participants, retention, sharing and transfers, and whether a DPIA is needed. Record each silence in the document as an evidence gap.
